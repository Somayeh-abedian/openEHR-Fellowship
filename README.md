# openEHR Fellowship Project

**Somayeh Abedian, PhD | openEHR Fellowship 2025–2026**

Welcome to my openEHR Fellowship project repository.

This repository documents my learning journey, professional reflections, activities, and outputs developed during the openEHR Fellowship Programme 2025–2026.

## About the Project

The Fellowship provides an opportunity to deepen engagement with the openEHR community, explore clinical information modelling and knowledge governance, and contribute to the development and reuse of interoperable health information.

My work draws on experience in national electronic health record implementation, health information standards, semantic interoperability, and digital health research.

## Repository Contents

- **About:** Background and context of the Fellowship project.
- **Aims and Objectives:** The intended goals and learning objectives.
- **Reflective Journals:** Reflections on activities, experiences, challenges, and lessons learned.
- **Documents:** Supporting materials and relevant resources.
- **Reports:** Documentation of project activities and outcomes.

## Areas of Interest

- openEHR clinical information modelling and archetypes
- Semantic interoperability and health data standards
- Reusable clinical knowledge and governance
- Personal Generated Health Data (PGHD)
- Integration across openEHR, HL7 FHIR, and OMOP
- Community collaboration and knowledge sharing
